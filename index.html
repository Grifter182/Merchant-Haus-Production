MerchantHaus Core Services

Innovative solutions designed to power modern commerce safely and efficiently across the globe.

Mobile Payments

Empower your customers with seamless and secure payment experiences on any device. Our mobile solutions support digital wallets, QR codes and in‑app purchases, making transactions faster and more convenient than ever before.

Advanced Fraud Detection

Protect your business and your customers with our multi‑layered fraud prevention system. Using machine learning and real‑time data analysis, we identify and block suspicious activities before they can cause harm, securing your revenue stream.

Modern POS Devices

Upgrade your in‑person checkout with our sleek and powerful Point‑of‑Sale terminals. Our devices accept all major payment types, including contactless, chip and swipe, ensuring a fast, reliable and user‑friendly transaction for every customer.

Global Payments

Expand your reach and accept payments from anywhere in the world. We handle multi‑currency processing, international compliance and local payment methods, providing a unified platform to manage your global commerce operations.

See Payment Solutions

Network Tokenization

Enhance data security and boost approval rates with network tokenization. By replacing sensitive card data with a unique token, we reduce the risk of data breaches and ensure smoother recurring payments and card‑on‑file transactions.

Ecommerce Solutions

Launch and grow your online store with our robust ecommerce platform. We provide secure payment gateways, customizable checkout pages and easy integration with popular shopping carts, giving you all the tools you need to succeed online.

Data & Analytics

Unlock powerful insights from your transaction data. Our analytics dashboard provides real‑time reporting, sales trends and customer behaviour analysis to help you make smarter, data‑driven decisions for your business.

Subscription Billing

Automate your recurring revenue with our flexible subscription management platform. Easily create and manage billing plans, handle prorated charges and reduce churn with smart dunning and automatic card updating features.

Chargeback Management

Win more disputes and recover lost revenue with our automated chargeback management system. We help you fight fraud‑related chargebacks by compiling compelling evidence and submitting it on your behalf, saving you time and money.

Payment Orchestration

Optimize your payment stack with our intelligent routing engine. Connect to multiple payment processors and dynamically route transactions to the best provider based on cost, approval rate or geography, maximizing your performance.
