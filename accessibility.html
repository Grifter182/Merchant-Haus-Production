Known Limitations

We believe in transparency. While we strive for full accessibility, some limitations currently exist. We are actively working to address these issues and improve our services.

🧩

Third-Party Integrations

▼

Our platform connects with various third-party tools. The accessibility of these external platforms is managed by their respective owners, and some may not fully support all assistive technologies. We actively audit and communicate with our partners to encourage improvements.

📊

Complex Reports & Dashboards

▼

Some of our complex, dynamically generated reports and data visualizations may have limited screen reader compatibility. To mitigate this, we strive to provide summary data tables and text-based descriptions as an alternative where possible.

📄

Legacy Documents

▼

Older documents, such as PDFs or emails created before our latest accessibility updates, may not meet all current guidelines. We are actively working to remediate and update these materials as they are identified or requested.
